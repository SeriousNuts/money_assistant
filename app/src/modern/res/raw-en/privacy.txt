Data handling:
• Sign-in, registration and cloud sync are disabled in this release. Previously created accounts and their data may remain on Firebase servers; the local version cannot access or delete them.
• Names, selected participants' phone numbers, bill titles, amounts, receipt items and payment states are used to split expenses and are stored on the device.
• Contacts access is requested only when choosing people. Names and phone numbers you select become local bill data. The broad READ_CONTACTS permission and picker need review for Google Play.
• Receipt photos and offline OCR remain in private on-device storage; photos are not synced to Firestore. If you separately confirm online OCR for a photo, a reduced copy is sent over HTTPS to Google Gemini. Google service terms and the plan tied to your own API key apply. Do not submit others' private receipts without a lawful basis; review the results.
• Your Gemini API key is protected locally with Android Keystore, removable in the app and not included in the APK. It is provided to Google for requests. Receipt images and the key are excluded from Android backup; other local data may be included when device backup is enabled.

The app does not include its own advertising or analytics SDKs. Firebase remains a dependency but configuration and sign-in are disabled; actual SDK behavior must be checked before completing Play Data safety. Optional Gemini transmissions and retention must also be checked. Sharing a bill via Android's Share menu is initiated by you and delivered to the recipient you choose.

Deletion: you may remove on-device data using Android system settings (“Clear storage”). Existing cloud accounts are not accessible in this release; clearing app storage does not delete them from the server. A verified server-side process and an external deletion-request page are needed BEFORE PUBLICATION. Retention periods and request handling: [SPECIFY AFTER IMPLEMENTATION].
