MONEY ASSISTANT PRIVACY POLICY (ALPHA VERSION)
Revision: November 7, 2026

This policy describes the alpha version of the app and is available at https://moneyassistantpolicy.duckdns.org/privacy/. Third-party online service terms may change; review the selected provider's terms before sending a photo.

Rights holder contact for data handling: Тепляков Илья Андреевич (Ilya Andreevich Teplyakov), Russian Federation; email: i@iteplyakov.ru; phone: +79232390866.

Data handling:
• This release does not offer accounts, sign-in or cloud sync. According to the rights holder, no real users had accounts in the earlier experimental Firebase configuration. The rights holder does not receive local bill data on its own server.
• Names, selected participants' phone numbers, bill titles, amounts, receipt items and payment states are used to split expenses and are stored on the device.
• Contacts access is requested only when choosing people. Names and phone numbers you select become local bill data. You can enter participants manually without granting contacts access.
• Receipt photos and offline OCR remain in private on-device storage; photos are not synced to Firestore. If you choose and separately confirm online OCR for a particular photo, a reduced copy is sent over HTTPS to the selected provider: Google Gemini (Google) or deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-OCR-2 via Cloud.ru (foundation-models.api.cloud.ru). The RuStore build preselects Cloud.ru but never sends a photo without separate confirmation. Verify the provider's terms and your lawful basis for transmitting other people's data. Availability, pricing, location, provider use and retention of data are governed by the selected provider and your plan; choosing Cloud.ru does not prove Russia-only storage or processing. Review all returned items and prices.
• Separate Gemini and Cloud.ru API keys are encrypted locally using Android Keystore, independently removable and not included in the APK. A key is transmitted over HTTPS only to its selected provider when making a request. Android app backup and device transfer are disabled for this release; this does not remove previously created backups, if any.

The app does not include its own advertising or analytics SDKs. Firebase remains a dependency but configuration and sign-in are disabled. Optional Gemini and Cloud.ru are used only by your choice; their processing and retention of submitted data follow the selected provider's terms. Sharing a bill via Android's Share menu is initiated by you and delivered to the recipient you choose.

Deletion and local retention: bills and photos stay on your device until you delete them in the app, clear its storage or uninstall it. There are no user accounts in this release, so there is no cloud account to delete. If you opt in to online OCR, the selected provider handles submitted data under its own terms; the rights holder does not control provider retention.
